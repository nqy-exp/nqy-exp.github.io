**Numbering Convention**: XX00-YYYY-LCGZ-00

**Format**: Sample ID-Treatment Mode (Phase 3 only)-Image Structure& mounting medium-Sequence Number

## Sample ID (XX):

- **T**: Test
- **CT**: Cotton Fiber
- **LE**: Linen Fiber
- **SK**: Silk Fiber
- **WO**: Wool Fiber
- **LY**: Lyocell Fiber
- **CU**: Cuprammonium Cellulose (Cupro)
- **VI**: Viscose Fiber
- **CA**: Cellulose Acetate Fiber
- **PT**: Polyester Fiber
- **PA**: Polyamide (Nylon) Fiber
- **BP**: Bamboo Fiber
- **M**: Mixed Fibers 
- **U**: Unknown Sample

*For single-letter identifiers, the format is X000.*

## Image Structure & Mounting Medium Abbreviations:

- **L**: Longitudinal
- **C**: Cross-sectional
- **G**: Glycerin
- **Z**: Nail coat
- **N**: Unembedded
